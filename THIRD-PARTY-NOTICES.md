# Third party notices

This extension ships the files listed below. Each keeps its own terms.

## The EU legal guarantee notice and the GARAN label

- Files: `assets/notice/*.png` and `assets/notice/*.pdf`
- Source: European Commission, *Practical guidelines and high resolution vector files of the EU
  notice and label on product guarantees*
  <https://commission.europa.eu/publications/practical-guidelines-and-high-resolution-vector-files-eu-notice-and-label-product-guarantees_en>
- Legal basis: Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/1960
  <https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:32025R1960>

These are official EU files, published so that traders can use them, and reuse of Commission
documents is governed by Decision 2011/833/EU. They are shipped **unchanged**: the regulation fixes
the text, the graphics and the colours of the notice, and this extension does not alter them. The
three editable fields of the GARAN label (duration, brand, model) are filled from the producer's
declaration that the merchant enters in the catalogue, which is what the guidelines require.

The PDFs are the Commission's *Harmonised notice in 24 languages colour and black and white*,
byte for byte, renamed from `Legal guarantee_notice SLN.pdf` to `legal-guarantee-notice-sl.pdf` so
the address has no space in it.

The English notice is not published as PNG by the Commission, only as SVG; the file shipped here
was rendered from that official SVG at the same size as the other languages, with nothing changed
in it.
